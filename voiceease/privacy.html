隐私政策 / Privacy Policy
生效日期 / Effective: 2026-10-31

我们如何处理录音

声息在获得麦克风权限后录音，在你的设备上分析人声。录音文件、个人声音基准、分数与历史记录保存在应用的本地存储中，供回放和趋势对比。开发者不会接收这些录音或分析结果，也不要求创建账户。卸载应用会移除其本地数据；请在卸载前自行留意需要保留的录音。

联网内容

应用会连接公开网站取得朗读短文和声音指南。中文内容来自国内网站，英文内容可来自 Wikipedia。访问这些网站时，网站可能按自己的政策接收 IP 地址、请求时间等常规网络信息。录音和分析结果不会随这些请求发送。

数据收集与追踪

我们没有广告或分析 SDK，不收集账户资料、位置、设备标识符、录音或使用记录，也不进行跨 App 追踪。

权限与选择

麦克风权限只用于你主动开始的录音。你可以随时在 iOS 设置中撤销权限；不联网时仍可使用应用内的固定绕口令，但在线文章可能无法刷新。

联系我们

如有隐私问题，请联系 bigtiger.wang@outlook.com。

How we handle recordings

With microphone permission, VoiceEase records and analyzes speech on your device. Audio files, your personal voice baseline, scores, and history are stored locally for playback and comparison. We do not receive them and do not require an account. Uninstalling the app removes its local data.

Online content

The app requests short reading passages and voice care articles from public websites. Chinese content comes from websites in China; English content may come from Wikipedia. Those sites may receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address and request time under their own policies. Your recordings and analysis results are never sent with these requests.

Collection and tracking

We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, collect personal data, or track you across apps.

Permissions and contact

Microphone access is used only for recordings you start. You can revoke it in iOS Settings. Questions: bigtiger.wang@outlook.com.
