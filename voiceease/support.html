倾听今天的声音状态

如何开始？

点“开始声音评测”，在弹窗内录制至少 10 秒声音。可以读屏幕上的短文，也可以读其他内容。先在状态较好时建立个人声音基准，以便日后比较。

录音保存在哪里？

录音与结果保存在本机。打开“记录”，点一条记录即可播放、暂停或停止，并查看分析结果。

为什么没有结果？

录音需要包含足够长的清晰人声片段。若时间不足或有效人声不足，应用会提示重录。评测结果仅用于观察个人趋势，不能诊断疾病。如持续声音嘶哑或不适，请咨询专业医疗人员。

文章无法刷新？

在线朗读素材和声音指南需要网络。请检查连接并重试；固定绕口令可以离线阅读。

Getting started

Tap “Start voice check” and record at least 10 seconds in the recording sheet. Read the suggested passage or your own words. Set a baseline when your voice feels good for future comparison.

Recordings and results

They stay on your device. Open History and select an entry to play, pause, stop, and review its analysis.

If no score appears

The recording needs a sufficiently long segment of usable speech. The app will ask you to try again if it is too short. Scores help track personal trends and are not a medical diagnosis. Seek professional care for persistent voice symptoms.

Online content

Reading passages and guide articles need internet access. Check your connection and try refreshing. Built-in tongue twisters work offline.
